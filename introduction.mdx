Pincast is a platform for real-world play. People walk to real places, and the apps built on Pincast give them a card for what they find there, such as a tree or a stop on a walk. Today the platform gives developers one API, the Placecard registry, at `https://api.pincast.fm/v1`. From your server, you register your app's card sets and cards, record each card a person collects, and read back what a person holds. People sign in through id.pincast.fm with one account for every Pincast app, and when a guest signs in, your app can move their cards to them. To get a test key, run `npm create pincast`, which needs no account, or make an app on the [dashboard](https://pincast.fm/developers/dashboard).
